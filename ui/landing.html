Six XAI Components

Every score comes with a full explanation, not just a number.

SH

Exact Shapley Values

Game-theoretic feature attribution satisfying efficiency, symmetry, dummy, and additivity axioms. Exact computation for small feature sets, Kernel SHAP with 512 permutations otherwise.

LI

LIME Local Explanations

300-perturbation weighted ridge regression validates every Shapley attribution locally. 100% directional agreement with SHAP in cross-validation.

CF

Counterfactual Analysis

Quantifies the exact score gain from acquiring each missing skill. Answers “what if I learn X?” with concrete score projections.

HM

Attention Heatmap

Token-level importance map highlighting which parts of your resume drive the match score. Combines ontology matches with TF-IDF weights.

IN

Feature Interactions

Detects skill synergies through Shapley interaction values. Identifies pairs that boost your score more than the sum of individual contributions.

NL

Natural Language Insights

Human-readable narratives generated from all attribution values. Turns dense metrics into actionable career guidance.

System Architecture

Five sequential layers transform a raw resume into a fully-explained match score.

Resume Text + Target Job Role | v +-------------------------+ | Resume Parser | Experience years, education, achievements +-------------------------+ | v +-------------------------+ | Ontology Engine | 22 canonical skills, 346 aliases | n-gram phrase matching | Parent-node credit propagation +-------------------------+ | v +-------------------------+ | TF-IDF + SVD (LSA) | 10,000 features (1-3 grams) | k = 150 components | 27.8% explained variance +-------------------------+ | v +-------------------------+ | Scoring Engine | Ontology 45% + Semantic 30% | Four weighted comps. | Depth 15% + Corpus 10% +-------------------------+ | v +-------------------------+ | XAI Layer | Shapley (exact for |F|<=12) | SHAP | LIME | CF | HT | Kernel SHAP (512 permutations) +-------------------------+ | v Dashboard Output
